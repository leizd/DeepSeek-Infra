## Overview

Version 4.9.1 tracks the Rust Storage and Transfer Plane cutover across 13 data and security domains.
The cutover is still in progress: Python remains the production storage authority until every phase below
has provider-backed evidence. The current native slices implement strict `object-set-v1`, Receipt v4,
Commit v4, signed replica-attestation verification, and the exact sovereign federation transfer journal
with SQLite durability and transactional CAS. Rust now also independently recomputes the complete
`predictive-planning-proof-v1` security binding instead of accepting declared PASS values. These slices
and the autonomous storage Evidence validator now recompute Receipt/Commit provider bytes and bindings.
The Rust proof plane also verifies the complete signed `federated-dr-proof-v1` recovery-capability,
production-restore, cleanup, and immutable-failure semantics, plus bilateral Fleet trust, signer rotation,
signed readiness, and challenge replay semantics. These slices do not yet move payload bytes, execute a
restore, or authorize remote effects. Rust now also reproduces the legacy recovery/process Evidence
validators without treating their static parity corpus as provider execution evidence.
The proof plane also validates retention safety, execution decisions, resilience scores, and
the provider-byte-bound destination of autonomous repair and rebalance.
Repair-worker crash Evidence now also checks process identity, increasing execution epochs,
stable repair-job identity, expired leases, and ordered reconciliation observations.
Blast-radius and atomic-budget Evidence now validates monotonic safety floors and
the observed one-admitted/one-rejected process race without granting control authority.
The remaining unpromoted risk, fairness, SLO, forecast, and federation planning claims
also have a separate Rust compatibility path; typed predictive proofs keep their stricter verification path.
Wave-crash Evidence now also binds outer schedule/wave/action leases, increasing runner epochs,
durable heartbeat observations, and exactly-once settlement without granting runner or worker authority.
Rust can now load an encrypted Fleet online-signer bundle and issue replica-attestation documents
without exposing private keys to Go. Multipart resume checkpoints now have a frozen Python/Rust
parity path. Replica-repair durable phase, cancel-before-effect, max-attempts, drain, and backoff
semantics now also have a frozen Python/Rust parity path. Replica-provision
`BackupReplicationJob` enqueue, claim, required/best-effort failure routing, and drain
classification now have a frozen Python/Rust parity path. Rebalance execute/drain prefix and
prune-safety hold matching now also have a frozen Python/Rust parity path. Target writer-lease
acquire, preempt, expiry, and ownership decisions now have a frozen Python/Rust parity path.
Recovery-copy Receipt/Commit authentication (missing/authenticated/conflicting/corrupt) now has a
frozen Python/Rust parity path. Replica lag, replication compliance, and maintenance-window
observations now have a frozen Python/Rust parity path. Transfer traffic-class matching, repair/rebalance
wave exclusion, P0 concurrency bypass, recovery-throttled class rates, and destination digest verify
now have a frozen Python/Rust parity path. Replica reconcile/rebalance planners (keyset cursor, scan,
needs-repair, and drain/capacity/failure-domain candidate selection) now have a frozen Python/Rust
parity path. These planners do not execute repairs or rebalance jobs. Payload-byte movement and
production storage authority remain Python.
