## 1. Executive Summary

DeepSeek Infra **4.6.9** delivers long-term sustainability and verifiable readiness for the Authority and Disaster Recovery control plane:

- **Authority History Retention Framework**: Safe, verifiable compaction that replaces immutable historical journal records with an anchor checkpoint while preserving exact mathematical replayability: `verify(C + tail) == verify(full history)`.
- **AuthorityCheckpoint v1**: Structured, deterministic, credential-free retention artifact binding `checkpointGeneration`, `ancestorDigest`, `headDigest`, `historyStartGeneration`, `includedMutationDigest`, `replicaCoverage`, and `formalTruthDigest`.
- **Fail-Closed Compaction Safety Gate**: RetentionJob state machine (`PENDING` → `VALIDATING` → `READY` → `EXECUTING` → `VERIFYING` → `COMMITTED`) strictly blocked on replica lag, cross-replica divergence/forks, active restore sessions, running DR rehearsals, stale formal truth, and unfinished GC.
- **Continuous DR Drill & Readiness Runner**: Background runner `run_dr_drill()` creating isolated temporary workspaces, executing real restore, verifying pre/post digests, Commit markers, Receipts, and Age payload, and producing `dr-readiness-proof-v1`.
- **Backup DR SLO Metrics**: Computes restore success rate (>= 99.9%), RTO p50/p95/p99, RPO, and evidence freshness (<= 7.0 days).
- **Authority History Snapshot & Explain API**: Rich operator diagnostics and structured blocking reasons for auditability.
- **Recovery Dependency Graph**: Tracks active receipt mutations and index coverage references across authority generations.
- **evidence-proof-v3**: Typed semantic validators for DR readiness and retention safety proofs.

All Backup wire formats remain frozen.
