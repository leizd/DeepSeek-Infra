**Applicable Version:** v4.7.4

## Development baseline

4.7.4 targets a durable, auditable, Evidence-closed autonomous operations layer
on top of 4.7.3's real storage effects: real Receipt/Commit byte proofs, complete
DAG wave scheduling, persistent risk and fair-service history, enforced transfer
budgets, safe preemption, and Fleet SLO/burn-rate/readiness reporting.

This file fixes the implementation and compatibility contract only. It does not
claim that Gates A-N pass. Those claims require successful exact-merge three-MinIO,
independent-process, Evidence Assembly, coverage, eval, and release gates.

## Frozen contracts

`object-set-v1`, Receipt v4, Commit v4, FastCDC v3, randomized Age, Projection
semantics, `control-authority-v1`, AuthorityCheckpoint v1, and
`dr-readiness-proof-v1` remain unchanged.
