**Applicable Version:** v4.7.4

## Implemented in this version

4.7.4 turns 4.7.3's real storage effects into a durable, auditable,
Evidence-closed autonomous operations layer:

- Autonomous proof reads the exact Receipt v4 and Commit v4 bytes back from the
  resolved S3/MinIO target and binds endpoint, bucket, keys, provider metadata,
  raw SHA-256 values, `Commit.receiptDigest`, and `objectSetDigest`. Synthetic
  digests and placeholder keys cannot satisfy semantic validation.
- The Storage Control Plane producer uploads the report and exact proof together.
  The report binds proof path, SHA-256, byte size, and scenario; producer staging
  and Evidence Assembly revalidate those same bytes and fail closed on absence or
  tampering.
- The real crash gate kills Worker A while a MinIO Repair is in
  `transferring-components`. Worker B must wait for the durable lease to expire,
  claim a higher `executionEpoch`, enter `RECONCILING`, find the existing
  `repairId`, and keep the underlying Repair job count at one.
- Two independent OS processes race against the same SQLite journal for global,
  target, policy, and failure-domain admission; each scope requires exactly one
  admission and one rejection.
- Exact RiskSubject `OPEN/CLEARED/REOPENED` lifecycle and policy virtual service
  history are durable. Risk Debt uses unresolved age, and production scheduling
  uses persisted virtual runtime/finish and actions/bytes served.
- Every candidate is assigned to a complete DAG wave or receives a typed
  `UNSCHEDULABLE` reason. `backup_transfer_budget` enforces Repair reserve, and
  safe preemption releases victim locks/budget and claims the critical Repair in
  one transaction.
- Blast simulation includes running effects plus proposed actions. Healthy
  baselines retain policy minima; already-degraded baselines cannot lose another
  committed copy or failure domain.
- `.resilience-slo/slo.sqlite3` persists detection/clear, queue, remediation, DR,
  takeover, starvation, and proof-freshness measurements. Configurable 1h/24h
  burn rates, maintenance overrides, and authenticated
  `GET /api/workspace/resilience/readiness` expose source-backed readiness.

A local real-three-MinIO developer run verifies the autonomous proof path, but it
is not release Evidence. Release readiness still requires the exact-merge Storage
Control Plane artifact, Evidence Assembly, 95% coverage, offline evals, and all
release gates.

See the
[Coordinated Autonomous Remediation runbook](../runbooks/COORDINATED_AUTONOMOUS_REMEDIATION.md)
for operations and forensic verification.

## Frozen contracts

`object-set-v1`, Receipt v4, Commit v4, FastCDC v3, randomized Age, Projection
semantics, `control-authority-v1`, AuthorityCheckpoint v1, and
`dr-readiness-proof-v1` remain unchanged.

## Explicit non-goals

This release does not introduce LLM-based autonomous decisions, automatic
primary promotion, automatic deletion, replication weakening, Raft,
multi-primary Authority, Receipt v5, Commit v5, or `object-set-v2`.
