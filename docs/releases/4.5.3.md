Release date: **2026-08-16**.

4.5.3 delivers **Replica Self-Healing & Lifecycle Governance (副本自愈与生命周期治理)** on the frozen
`object-set-v1` / Receipt v4 / Commit v4 / randomized Age wire formats. It transitions the replica set from
one-shot replication into a continuously converging desired-state self-healing architecture.

## Highlights

1. **Durable Replication Retry & Safe Spool Retention**  
   Transient required replica failures transition to `retry-wait` with backoff and `repair-needed`. Local encrypted spool is retained until durable repair intent exists.

2. **Replication Orchestration Visibility**  
   Orchestration errors are surfaced directly in DR readiness and ledger (`replicationCompliance: degraded`, `replica-enqueue-failed`) rather than swallowed silently.

3. **Remote DR Audit: Raw-Bytes Verification & Commit Chain Continuity**  
   Audits `receiptDigest` against raw receipt bytes and validates `targetGeneration` continuity, `previousCommitHash` chain, genesis root, and `control/head.json`.

4. **Policy Target Bindings & Atomic Drill Selection**  
   Policy rejects unregistered replica target bindings before persistence; scheduled drill selects `(targetId, backupId, objectSetDigest)` tuples atomically.

5. **Ciphertext-Plane Self-Healing (`ReplicaReconciler` & `ReplicaRepairJob`)**  
   Desired-state reconciler triggers zero-decrypt, zero-encrypt ciphertext transfers from authenticated healthy source copies; source holds protect sources before reading; missing components are healed in place; destination corruption is quarantined.

6. **Target-Local Replica Catalogs & Mirrored Retention**  
   Replicas append receipts to target-local catalogs; retention mirrors primary across replicas with a hard `minCommittedCopies` safety gate; retired recovery points are never repaired.

7. **Deterministic Lexicographic Recovery Planner**  
   Lexicographic ranking (health, preference, cache coverage, drill freshness, scrub freshness, calibrated RTO, stable tiebreak) with target-specific calibrated RTO and zero remote I/O.

8. **Replica Lag Telemetry & Objectives**  
   Per-target lag telemetry (`lagRecoveryPoints`, `lagSeconds`) and policy objective evaluation (`maxReplicaLagSeconds`).

## Compatibility

- Frozen wire formats: `object-set-v1`, Receipt v4, Commit v4, FastCDC v3, randomized Age encryption.
- Zero Age decrypts and zero Age encrypts during self-healing replication and repair.
