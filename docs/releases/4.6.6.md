## 1. Executive Summary

DeepSeek Infra **4.6.6** wires 4.6.5 Control Authority contracts into production S3/MinIO disaster recovery:

- Production `AuthorityStoreFactory` from secretless `credentialReference`
- **Configured ≠ resolved** fail-closed (never degrade to local-only ACTIVE)
- **Mandatory Formal Truth** before ACTIVE (no production activation bypass)
- S3 lagging-ancestor anti-entropy with observable repair outcomes
- Explicit acknowledgement durability policy (`minDurableReplicas`)
- Evidence integrity: filesystem fresh-process ≠ three-MinIO claims
- Operator `authority_health_snapshot` / `authority_verify`

Wire formats remain frozen.
