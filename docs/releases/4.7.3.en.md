## 1. Executive Summary

DeepSeek Infra **4.7.3** advances autonomous resilience to **Proof-Carrying Fleet Resilience & Production Coordination**:

- **Genuine Multi-MinIO E2E Verification & Evidence**: Completely purges filesystem target mocking, mock replica execution, and manual ledger writes. Validates 11 autonomous resilience capabilities against real MinIO clusters, real Age cryptography, and production S3TargetStore.
- **Crash-Recoverable Transactions & Effect Reconciliation**: Following worker crash or lease preemption, persistent `effectHandle` inspections arbitrate recovery directives (`RESUME`, `VERIFY`, `RECREATE`, `COMPENSATE`, or `EFFECT_UNKNOWN`), preventing blind replay.
- **Fleet Scheduler & Multi-Dimensional Risk Debt Model**: Quantifies risk debt across severity, age factor, policy criticality, and SLO breach risk; applies Weighted Fair Queueing to eliminate starvation.
- **Safe-Point Preemption & Bandwidth Reservation**: Preemption is strictly confined to non-destructive safe points (`PENDING`, `CLAIMED`). Explicit `repairReserveBandwidth` protects high-priority repairs from being starved by low-priority rebalances.
- **Real Blast-Radius Simulation**: Calculates transient copy count and failure domain integrity across running effects and proposed waves to prevent concurrent degradation.
