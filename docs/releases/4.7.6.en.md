**Applicable Version:** v4.7.6

4.7.6 connects the predictive, optimizer, and Wave models from 4.7.5 to the
production control loop. Callers no longer self-report freshness, actual service,
or zero mutation.

## Closed loop

- Wave admission only accepts a production fresh-state bundle. Missing Authority,
  complete RiskSnapshot, capacity, running effects, budgets, maintenance, or blast
  simulation fails closed.
- The durable Wave runner executes Action Journal effects directly, fences crash
  takeover with monotonic epochs, and reuses existing Repair/Rebalance/Drill effects.
- Fair service settles exactly once from terminal effect telemetry; callers cannot
  provide actual bytes or duration.
- The maintenance loop records incarnation-isolated capacity observations from real
  target probes. MinIO/S3 uses the provider's paginated object inventory. The
  Forecast Registry persists 30/90-day records and automatically backtests them.
- `/whatif` accepts only hypothetical candidates. The service builds baseline,
  forecast, price catalog, Authority, effects, and maintenance truth internally.
- A write-deny capability blocks Storage, Authority, Action Journal, Policy, and
  Target mutations. Attempt audit and independently measured pre/post digests prove
  the result.
- `predictive-planning-proof-v1` is a typed payload in the unchanged
  `evidence-proof-v2` envelope. Assembly requires report, autonomous proof, and
  predictive proof exact artifacts.
- A real three-MinIO scenario runs Age Backup, Repair, Rebalance, capacity sampling,
  automatic backtesting, and What-If, then requeries all inventories for equality.
- Federation remains read-only and rejects tampered, stale/future, same-fleet, and
  wire-incompatible snapshots. Signing and cross-fleet writes are not introduced.

## Evidence status

A local real-three-MinIO predictive proof is development evidence only. Formal
release PASS still requires all three exact artifacts from the final merge SHA's CI
producer and successful global Evidence Assembly.

## Wire freeze

object-set-v1, Receipt v4, Commit v4, FastCDC v3, randomized Age,
control-authority-v1, AuthorityCheckpoint v1, dr-readiness-proof-v1, and the
evidence-proof-v2 envelope remain unchanged.

## Explicit non-goals

Cross-fleet writes, remote autonomous Repair, remote policy mutation, signed
federation, multi-primary Authority, consensus/Raft, automatic replica deletion,
durability reduction, primary promotion, and protocol version bumps.
