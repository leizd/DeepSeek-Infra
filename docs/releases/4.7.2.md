## 1. Executive Summary

DeepSeek Infra **4.7.2** upgrades autonomous resilience from single-action durable dispatch into **Coordinated Autonomous Remediation & Crash-Recoverable Execution** (*Verified Effects, Multi-Risk Conflict Resolution & Blast-Radius Safety*):

- **Immutable Plan & Action Identity (Gate A)**: Plan and action identities are create-once immutable entities. Identical ID + identical content replays idempotently without state mutation; conflicting content fails closed (409 Conflict). INSERT OR REPLACE is purged from the correctness path so SUCCEEDED actions can never be reset to PENDING.
- **Crash-Recoverable Action Leases & CAS Fencing (Gate B)**: CLAIMED, EXECUTING, VERIFYING, and RECONCILING actions carry xecutionEpoch, claimToken, and renewable leaseUntil. Stale workers attempting to commit after lease expiration are fenced out via CAS (WHERE action_id=? AND execution_epoch=? AND claim_token=?).
- **Effect Reconciliation Engine (Gate C)**: Persistent ffectHandle tracking allows newly claimed workers to inspect and recover in-flight subsystem tasks without blind re-execution, and introduces the first-class EFFECT_UNKNOWN state for uncertain remote mutations.
- **All-Subsystem Idempotency (Gate D)**: 
un_dr_drill, create_repair_job, xecute_replica_repair, create_rebalance_job, and xecute_rebalance_job natively bind 
esilienceActionId to ensure duplicate executions return the existing task.
- **Real Outcome Contracts & Rebalance Completion (Gate E)**: Rebalance requires full ciphertext transfer and authenticated destination copy (Receipt/Commit binding); Repair requires durable committed replica and failure domain verification. Rebalance cannot succeed merely on jobId creation.
- **Scoped Risk Effect Verification (Gate F)**: Dynamically derives ffectObserved = (severityAfter < severityBefore) for the targeted 
iskSubject. Unchanged or worsened risk halts the action and fails the outcome gate.
- **Planner Candidate Selection Correctness (Gate G)**: Purges improper fallbacks to unrelated backup recovery points during capacity candidate copy selection.
- **Multi-Risk Coordination DAG (Gate H)**: Organizes complex multi-risk environments into ResilienceCoordinationPlan v1, tracking dependencies (e.g. repair replica before drain) and conflict graphs.
- **Durable Resource Locks (Gate I)**: SQLite-backed resource locks serialize concurrent mutations on (policyId, backupId, targetId) tuples.
- **Atomic Safety Budgets (Gate J)**: Global and per-target concurrency and bandwidth admission are evaluated and committed in a single atomic transaction. Critical repairs preempt warning rebalances when slots are exhausted.
- **Blast-Radius Invariant Enforcement (Gate K)**: Ensures active coordination plans never drop available copies below minCommittedCopies or failure domains below minFailureDomains.
- **Effect-Aware Compensation & Operator Intervention (Gate L)**: Distinguishes NO_EFFECT, CANCELABLE, COMPENSATABLE, EFFECT_UNKNOWN, and NEEDS_OPERATOR.

All storage wire protocols and control authority checkpoints remain strictly frozen and compatible.
