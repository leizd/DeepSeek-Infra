**Applicable Version:** v4.7.5

**Subtitle:** 30/90-Day Capacity Forecasting, Cost-Aware Placement & Side-Effect-Free What-If Planning

4.7.5 upgrades 4.7.4's Evidence-Closed Autonomous Operations into verifiable
Predictive Fleet Planning without letting an optimizer execute destructive
decisions from instantaneous state.

## Closed loop

- Risk observations reconcile absent subjects against authoritative snapshot coverage.
- The scheduler separates service reservation from consumed fair share.
- The durable wave executor releases Wave N only after Wave N-1 verified success
  and revalidates Authority, risk, budget, and blast radius first.
- Capacity forecasts use durable observations for 30/90-day P50/P90 headroom;
  insufficient samples return INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
- Placement optimization uses a digest-bound price catalog and rejects cheaper
  plans that reduce minCommittedCopies or minFailureDomains.
- What-If simulation proves zero storage, Authority, and journal mutation.
- Federation is credential-free, digest-bound, and read-only.

object-set-v1, Receipt v4, Commit v4, FastCDC v3, randomized Age,
control-authority-v1, AuthorityCheckpoint v1, dr-readiness-proof-v1, and the
